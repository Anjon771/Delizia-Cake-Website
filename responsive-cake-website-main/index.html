Our Culinary Philosophy · Established in Miraflores

Our Passion For Baking Cakes

We are a bakery specializing in homemade desserts, custom cakes, brownies, and a wide variety of gourmet sweets. Our passion for baking allows us to create perfect sweet experiences for celebrations, special events, or simply to enjoy anytime.

01. Traceable Peruvian Ingredients

We partner directly with family orchards in Huaral for strawberries and cooperativas in Quillabamba for aromatic fine-flavor cacao.

02. French Pâtisserie Technique

Our Swiss meringue buttercreams and mousses contain 35% less refined sugar than conventional bakery frostings for a clean, balanced finish.
